Abstract - Gradious Pay

Gradious Pay is a advanced digital payment application similar to other Upi apps(Google Pay...) . Users can send and receive money through their UPI wallets in a secure manner. The app aims to make payment transactions, history, and bank account management, and make it simple and effortless. The application is designed with primary focus on the students, young professionals and small vendors who wish to make quick and secure digital payments Gradious Pay enables users to easily navigate through the app which does not require high tech skills.

The whole process of developing the app will start with designing the frontend. Core functionality pages with login UI, dashboard, transaction history, and send/receive money screens will be developed through HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It will be followed by implementing the backend with Node.js and managing user data, transactions, and security with MySQL. Finally, for user's convenience the static frontend will be replaced with more attractive alternative of React.js that allows real time updates and smooth change interactions.

We are adding capabilities to Gradious Pay that are uncommon in other UPI apps in order to make it more distinctive. Through visual expense analytics, users will be able to see insightful information about their spending and classify their payments using personalized tags and notes. Support for group payments will make it simple for users to divide expenses with friends or family. Additionally, the app will provide intelligent reminders for routine payments, such as tuition fee or rent. A special student mode will provide a more straightforward interface and budgeting tools specifically designed for students, while an integrated reward point system will promote active usage. and if any possible features or anything will be looking into it
