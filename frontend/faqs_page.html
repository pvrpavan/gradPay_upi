FAQ

How do I send money?
Open the app → Tap 'To Other Account' → Enter UPI ID or select contact → Enter amount → Send.
How do I check balance?
Tap Account Balance, select bank, and verify with UPI PIN. Your balance appears instantly.
How does the tracker work?
It auto-categorizes your expenses and shows them in graphs. You can set budgets too.
How to scan a QR code?
Use the bottom “Scanner” tab. Grant camera access and scan any UPI-compatible QR.
Can I add more banks?
Yes, in “Choose Bank” you can link, switch, or delete UPI-enabled accounts anytime.
Is Gradious Pay safe?
Yes! Your PIN/OTP are handled only by banks. We follow full UPI security guidelines.
